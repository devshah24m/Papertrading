How the arena works

  • Every trader starts with ₹1,00,000. Ranking is by equity = cash + open P&L, so charges and slippage hurt exactly like in real life.
  • MIS (intraday) gets 5x leverage and long or short is allowed. All MIS positions are squared off automatically at 3:15 PM IST.
  • CNC (delivery) needs full cash, long only, and can be carried overnight.
  • Trading hours follow NSE: Mon-Fri, 9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST.
  • Everyone can see every trader's equity, return and open scrips.

How fills are matched

  • Orders are matched against the live 5-level market depth from Angel One. If 1,000 shares are offered at the best ask and you buy 2,000, the first 1,000 fill there and the rest walk up to the next levels.
  • Beyond the visible book, price keeps stepping up (or down for sells) by about 5 bps per level, so very large orders pay real impact. Circuit limits cap the walk.
  • Limit orders are immediate-or-cancel: they fill what the book offers at or better than your price, and the rest is cancelled.
  • Your average price, slippage and every charge are shown in the ticket before you send the order.
